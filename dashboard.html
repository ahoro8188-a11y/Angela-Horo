A Note on Scope

Why this matters for HR reading

NCWA is negotiated centrally between CIL management and five recognised trade unions through the JBCCI, and applies uniformly to non-executive cadres across all CIL subsidiaries — CCL's HR function administers it rather than designs it. Executive-cadre staff are largely governed by a separate IDA pay pattern, which is why coverage in the chart alongside drops sharply for that group.
