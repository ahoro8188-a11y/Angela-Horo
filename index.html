About me

I belong to Ranchi, Jharkhand — a city that taught me, long before any classroom did, that people remember how you made them feel, not just what you delivered for them. Community there wasn't an HR buzzword; it was just what neighbours did for each other on an ordinary Sunday.

That instinct followed me into every room I've walked into since — lecture halls, internships, client calls for the website business I run on the side. I notice who's staying quiet in a meeting. I ask the second question, not just the first. Somewhere along the way, I realised organisations need exactly that same instinct — just pointed at people decisions instead of neighbours.

I'm still that person from Ranchi. I just happen to read compensation data now too.
